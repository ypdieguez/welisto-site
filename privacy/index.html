Privacy policy

Welisto · Effective November 1, 2026

In short: Welisto keeps everything you enter on your device. There's no account, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising. Crash reports are sent to help us fix bugs, and you can turn them off. Anonymous usage data is sent only if you agree. Neither ever includes what you type.

What stays on your device

Welisto stores these only on your phone, inside the app:

  • Your lists, items, quantities, prices and templates.
  • The name and profile photo you add, and photos you give templates.
  • Your settings, such as appearance and currency.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include the app's data if you have backups turned on. That backup is controlled by you and your device's settings, not by Welisto.

Crash reports

When Welisto crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a service that stores it for us in the European Union. A report contains the error and where in Welisto's code it happened, the app version, your phone's model and operating system version, when the app was opened, and a random ID the crash reporter creates on your phone, so we can tell how often it crashes and for how many installs. That ID isn't linked to your name or any account.

A report never includes your list, item or template names, prices, your name or photos, what you tap or type, your IP address or your location. Crash reports are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share crash reports.

Usage data

If you agree, in the "Help improve Welisto" prompt or in Settings › Privacy › Share usage data, Welisto sends anonymous usage data to PostHog, a service that stores it for us in the European Union. Nothing is sent unless you agree.

Usage data covers:

  • Which screens you open, and when the app is opened and closed.
  • Actions such as finishing setup, creating a list or template, adding an item, and completing, archiving or deleting a list, with counts such as how many items a list has.
  • The appearance, language and currency you choose.
  • The app version, your phone's model and operating system, and its language.

It's tied to a random ID created on your phone when you agree, not to your name or any account. It never includes your list, item or template names, prices, your name or photos, or your IP address. Turning usage data off stops sending it and deletes that ID from your phone; turning it on again starts a new one.

What we don't collect

Welisto has no account or sign-in, no advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites, and it doesn't share your data with anyone besides the two services above. It doesn't use your location, contacts or advertising ID.

Camera and photos

Welisto asks for the camera, or opens your photo library, only when you choose to add a profile or template photo. The photo you pick is copied into the app on your device. Welisto doesn't read or upload anything else from your library.

When you send feedback

"Send feedback" in Settings opens your own email app with a new message to us. If you send it, we receive your email address, your message, and the app version and device platform that the message includes. We use them only to reply to you and to improve Welisto. We delete your messages if you ask us to.

Deleting your data

You can delete lists, items, templates and photos in the app at any time. Uninstalling Welisto deletes all of its data from your device. Crash reports and usage data are kept for a limited time and then deleted; because they aren't linked to you, we can't look up yours, but turning them off in Settings stops anything new from being sent.

Children

Welisto isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If what Welisto does with your information changes, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
